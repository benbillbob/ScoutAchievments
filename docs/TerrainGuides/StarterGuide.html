Step 1 · Website

Start at the official Terrain website

Open Terrain in a modern web browser on your computer, tablet or phone.

terrain.scouts.com.au ↗

Step 2 · Login

Log in with your Scouts details

Select your Branch, then enter your member number and password.

Can’t remember your password?
Try the help links on the login page. If you still cannot get in, contact a Scout Leader (try the WhatsApp Group for the quickest response) and ask them to help reset your password.

Step 3 · Basecamp

Basecamp is your starting point

After logging in, find the three buttons under the starting island. Begin with the first two.

Basecamp→Introduction to Scouting→Introduction to Section

Step 4 · Program Essentials

Complete the two introductions

Go through Introduction to Scouting, then Introduction to Section.

✓Open each item in both introductions.
✓When an item has a video or content link, help your Scout open it and view the content together.
✓Talk about what it means and let your Scout ask questions.

Terrain may look a little different depending on your Scout’s age section and Branch.

Step 5 · Questions & review

You do not need to know everything today

If you or your Scout would like more information about any section, note it down rather than getting stuck.

Bring your notes along

We will review the introductions, answer questions and help your Scout with the next steps at the next opportunity during one of our weekly meetings.
