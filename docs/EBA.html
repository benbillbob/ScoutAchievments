A Better EBA — Built by All of Us

More Members. More Power. A Better EBA.

The improvements we want are possible—but only if more of us stand together

Before we can push for an EBA, we need to grow our union.

First, build our power

An EBA Starts With Membership

  • More members give us a stronger collective voice
  • Strong membership shows our priorities are widely shared
  • Visible support gives us confidence to begin bargaining
  • Every new member makes it harder to ignore us

The EBA is the goal. Membership is how we get there.

What stronger membership can win

What We Are Fighting For

  • Protect our jobs and conditions
  • Keep pay moving with living costs
  • Make flexibility fair and dependable
  • Support people through work and life
  • Give employees a genuine say

These outcomes need collective bargaining—and collective bargaining needs members.

Fair pay

Everyone should know what their work is worth

  • Annual rises that keep pace with inflation
  • Transparent pay bands for every role
  • Consistent pay principles across Australia
  • Salary protected from uncertain equity

Fair pay takes bargaining power. Bargaining power comes from membership.

Job security

Security When Change Happens

  • Stronger redundancy pay based on service
  • Clear timelines, consultation and fewer repeated rounds
  • Genuine redeployment opportunities
  • Equity protection and no post-redundancy non-competes

Together, we can insist employees do not carry all the risk.

Flexibility

Flexibility We Can Rely On

  • Remote work by default where the role allows
  • Office attendance only for operational reasons
  • Fair access to remote contracts and unpaid leave
  • Practical part-time options for returning primary carers

A right with clear rules—not a favour that can disappear.

Work and life

A modern workplace must support modern families

  • Meaningful parental and carer leave
  • Support for miscarriage, premature birth, fertility and adoption
  • Help with care costs caused by office attendance
  • Practical wellbeing, ergonomic and study support

No one should have to choose between their job, health and family.

Fair performance

Human Decisions, Clear Standards

  • Realistic and measurable expectations
  • Transparent ratings, benchmarks and pay links
  • Protection from unfair performance management
  • Human review and safeguards whenever AI is used

Accountability must apply to the system—not only the employee.

Protect our conditions

Protect What We Already Have

  • Existing leave and flexibility
  • Fair overtime, callout and time-in-lieu arrangements
  • Clear communication during organisational change
  • Consistent processes for everyone

If a benefit matters, it belongs in the agreement.

Employee voice

Decisions about us should not be made without us

Meaningful union consultation on:

  • Restructures and redundancies
  • Pay and performance systems
  • AI-assisted employment decisions
  • Major changes to conditions

A bigger union means a stronger voice before decisions are final—not after.

The core deal

Our Priorities

  1. Pay that keeps up
  2. Security during change
  3. Flexibility with clear rules
  4. Support for families and wellbeing
  5. Transparent, fair decisions
  6. A genuine employee voice

A fairer workplace with protections we can count on.
